## Why we collect information about work and security

[Company] collects limited information to keep company and customer data safe, to run its systems, and to see how assigned work is progressing. We do not use this information to rank employees, and no employment decision is made automatically from it.

## What applies to you depends on the device

- **Server workspace (your remote desktop):** we record when you sign in and out, workspace start and end, security events from the server, and your export requests.
- **Company laptop:** a disclosed service reports device health (updates, encryption, whether the security agent is running). You can see what is installed on the "What is collected about me" page.
- **Your personal computer:** we do not install anything on it and we do not inspect it. We see only that you opened a remote session and what you do in company applications.

## What we collect

Tasks, deliverable links and blockers; your daily update, which you write; project time only if you enter it; sign-in and workspace events; device health for company devices; export requests and approvals; security cases opened about events on company systems.

## What we never collect

Window titles, full web addresses, clipboard contents, screenshots or recordings, keystrokes, passwords, personal browsing, camera or microphone.

## When

Security events are recorded whenever you use company systems. Work updates are collected when you write them. [Add schedule for any optional category.]

## Who can see it

You can see your own records. Your direct manager sees your work summaries. The security team sees security events and export requests. Administrators can access systems through separate accounts, and their actions are logged.

## How long we keep it

Security and session records [90 days]; approval and privileged-action records [180 days]; work summaries [12 months]; optional activity data, if ever enabled, [30 days]. Records under a legal hold are kept until the hold ends. [Confirm for each country.]

## Checking and correcting

Open "My records" to see what we hold. You can add a correction to your work updates; the original stays in the history. For other corrections, or to raise a concern, contact [NAME, ROLE, EMAIL].

## Security incidents

If a security event involves your account or device, a person reviews it before any action is taken against you. Security evidence is kept separate from work assessment.

## Limits

We cannot prevent every way information could leave, for example photographing a screen, and we do not claim to.
